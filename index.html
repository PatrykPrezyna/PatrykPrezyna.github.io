New machine generation · Systems engineering · Rommelag

bottelpack bp 700 Series

A new Blow-Fill-Seal generation, brought in as one system.

  • The bottelpack bp 700 Series is Rommelag’s Blow-Fill-Seal platform for large-volume parenterals. One machine forms, fills, and seals bottles from 100 to 1,000 ml. Systems engineering on this line was that span: from the automation through the packaging equipment, the devices that produce the container, and the molding tools that travel with each format.
  • The series is built to raise output and shrink the cost around the machine. A bp 730 nearly doubles bottles per hour versus the bp 321 at 1,000 ml. A format change is under two hours.
  • Only 20% of the aseptic cabinet sits in the classified area, so the line fits a 3.5 m room. Cleaning, recipes, and the guided operator interface are part of the same system, on GAMP category 4 software.

Robotics · Knee replacement · Architecture

Knee replacement robotic assistant

From stakeholder needs to the concept that was selected.

  • A new product generation of a surgical robot for knee reconstruction. I worked the system architecture from stakeholder needs through to the concept that was selected: what each group required, how those needs decomposed into requirements, and the tradeoffs that decided the design.
  • The surgeon is the primary user. Patients, the operating room, hospitals, regulators, and insurers set the constraints around that user: a safe procedure, surgeon control, a smaller footprint, and less time to set up and clear the room.
  • Those needs became requirements. Cuts within 1 mm. End-to-end latency under 100 ms. Setup and cleanup under 45 minutes. Weight under 7 lb.
  • The tradespace compared how the robot is mounted, powered, and navigated, and how much of the cut it controls. Handheld concepts score higher on performance for the manufacturing cost.
  • The concept carried forward is a wireless handpiece: robotic control of position and the cutting plane, inertial navigation with an infrared camera as backup, the battery on the tool, and the computation off the device.

Concept selection. Highlighted options are the architecture carried forward, from control and mount through power and computing.
